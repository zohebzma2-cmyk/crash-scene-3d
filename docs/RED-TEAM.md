## Things that held up

- Speed input: 151 and 99999999999999999999 -> "That's faster than we can illustrate. Enter 150 mph or less."; -5 -> "can't be below 0 mph"; 25.55 -> "Use whole or tenths"; `abc`, `1e2` and Arabic-Indic digits -> "should be a number of mph"; surrounding spaces trimmed; 150 accepted and produced an off-road warning.
- Refusals: same direction with no rear hit, and head-on with a rear hit, each got a plain sentence plus "Fix the details" / "See it anyway". Both parked: "At least one vehicle has to be moving", with no "See it anyway". 4 vehicles: Add is disabled after D; C and D without directions are refused ("Pick which way vehicle C was going.").
- Jumping to `#export` by setting the hash with an invalid scene sent the app back to `#location`; browser back/forward and reload mid-flow ended on a valid step with no page errors.
- Every step at 1440 and 390 shows the short disclaimer; "Learn more" shows the long one; the PDF footer and JSON `disclaimer` carry the long one; every PNG footer carries the short one with timestamp and fingerprint.
- Fingerprint: `shasum -a 256` of the saved JSON matched the 64 characters printed in the PDF; editing one number changed the hash. PDF, PNGs and the "Saved" line shared one timestamp and fingerprint.
- Tampered files: a replaced `disclaimer` and a forged `derived` (speedUsedMph 60, warnings removed) were ignored; re-saving restored the standard disclaimer and recomputed values. Refused with plain messages: 1.1 MB file, invalid JSON, `zone: "roof"`, speed 500, 6 vehicles.
- Text injection: `<img src=x onerror=alert(1)>` in make and `<script>` in claim number rendered as text; no dialog fired; no page errors. PDF wrote ≈ and → as words and printed unsupported glyphs (emoji, Ω) as "?".
- Changing the claimant's own make/model is labelled "changed from your claim" while the header keeps the claim vehicle.
- Phone layout: no horizontal page scroll (scrollWidth 390) on review, measure, aftermath and vehicles; map tiles extend past the edge but are clipped.
- Keyboard: all review controls reachable by Tab with names (including the 3D canvas, "Illustration of the crash ..."); arrow keys move between hit-zone radios.
- No requests left localhost:4190 during any run.
